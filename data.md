# Appalachian Economy — Key Data Sources

### 1. Appalachian Regional Commission (ARC)

[ARC Data](https://data.arc.gov/data?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
Provides county-level Appalachian economic and socioeconomic data, including income, poverty, employment, population, and economic status.

### 2. U.S. Census Bureau — American Community Survey (ACS)

[ACS Data](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
Provides detailed county-level demographic, income, education, employment, housing, and migration data for analyzing economic differences across Appalachia.

### 3. Bureau of Labor Statistics — Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages (QCEW)

[QCEW Data](https://www.bls.gov/cew/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
Provides county-level employment and wage data by industry, allowing analysis of industrial structure and economic changes over time.

### 4. USDA — Census of Agriculture

[Census of Agriculture](https://www.nass.usda.gov/AgCensus/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
Provides county-level information on farms, agricultural production, farm size, sales, livestock, crops, and farm operators.

### 5. Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA)

[GDP by County](https://www.bea.gov/data/gdp/gdp-county?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
Provides county-level GDP, personal income, earnings, employment, and industry data to measure economic performance and structural changes.
