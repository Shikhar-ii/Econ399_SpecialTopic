## Overview

This repository contains work completed for **ECON 399**, an independent research study conducted under the guidance of **Dr. Sam** at West Virginia State University.

The research focuses on the **Appalachian region**, examining economic conditions, development, communities, and regional trends through data-driven research and analysis.

## Research Focus

* Economic development in Appalachia
* Regional economic trends
* Employment and labor markets
* Population and demographic changes
* Industry and economic opportunities
* Data-driven analysis of Appalachian communities

## Course Structure

This independent study emphasizes:

* Independent research
* Economic data collection and analysis
* Literature and regional research
* Statistical analysis and visualization
* Research writing and presentation
* Understanding economic challenges and opportunities within Appalachia

## Repository Contents

This repository will contain research materials, datasets, analysis, visualizations, notes, and final outputs developed throughout the ECON 399 independent study.
